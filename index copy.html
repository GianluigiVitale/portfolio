DriftBench: Measuring and Predicting
Infrastructure Drift in LLM Serving Systems
Universitas Mercatorum 236,985 prompt-response pairs MIT License · CC BY 4.0
A measurement framework and predictive model (PRI) for infrastructure drift in LLM serving systems. Demonstrates a 23.85% safety flip rate on H100→B200 migration. Three self-contained evaluation paths: GPU production case study, CPU-only PRI retraining, and automated numerical verification of all 34 claims.
