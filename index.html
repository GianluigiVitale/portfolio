01 / Selected work

Measure the stack. Explain the change.

My work connects infrastructure-level changes to observable differences in model behavior, with an emphasis on reproducible evidence.

MLSys 2026Oral presentationSole author

DriftBench: Measuring and Predicting Infrastructure Drift in LLM Serving Systems

A benchmark and predictive model for measuring how GPU architecture, numerical precision, and inference-framework changes silently alter LLM outputs. Across 237,000 evaluations, hardware-induced drift proved highly predictable while framework-induced drift generally required direct re-measurement.
